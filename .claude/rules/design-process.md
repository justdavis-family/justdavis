# Design Process

New features and significant enhancements should follow a structured design process,
  running from analysis and product vision through to code.
These stages are documented in detail in the repository's root `design/` directory.

For full details on the design and development process, see: @design/README.md

To be walked through the process step by step — which document comes next,
  and where its rules and template live — invoke the
  [`design-process` skill](/.claude/skills/design-process/SKILL.md).
